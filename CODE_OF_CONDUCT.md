# Participation principles

Discuss evidence and interpretations respectfully. Make space for clinical, technical, data, operational and patient perspectives. Challenge claims without personal attacks. Declare relevant interests and distinguish personal views from organisational positions.

Do not disclose confidential or personal health information. Obtain agreement before attributing another person's contribution. Moderators may remove unsuitable content and explain the reason proportionately.

Before launch, appoint a conduct contact and agree a confidential reporting and escalation route. Do not report sensitive conduct matters through public issues. This starter does not invent an operational reporting service.
