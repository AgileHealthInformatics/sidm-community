## Question

Can a transparent AI-assisted method help practitioners identify and assess differences in data definitions across a small, agreed set of specifications?

## Scope to agree

Select one information concept and two or three specifications that participants may lawfully process. Record exact editions, relevant clauses and the intended use of each specification. Choose the concept and sources with the participating community before analysis starts.

## Participation

A convenor frames the question, a facilitator prepares the analysis and reviewers check the sources. Contributors may verify one candidate finding or explain its implementation significance. Named participants and dates have not yet been agreed.

## Method

1. Agree the source set and permissions; retain a source register.
2. Extract relevant definitions with their context and exact references.
3. Ask AI to identify candidate differences, including scope and intended-use differences.
4. Review every candidate against the original sources.
5. Classify findings as confirmed, rejected or unresolved, retaining the rationale.
6. Publish a bounded synthesis, limitations and reusable method.

## Intended output

A reviewed evidence table and short practical account of where the method helped or failed. Record reviewer effort and corrections so that efficiency claims can be assessed.
