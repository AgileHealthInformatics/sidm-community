This starter site contains no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, third-party fonts or embedded media. Links to external services take visitors to those services, whose own practices apply. Hosting providers may process technical request information; the publishing organisation should review and complete its privacy notice before launch.

Public issues, pull requests and commit history may reveal contributor names and contributions. Contributors should submit only material suitable for public disclosure. Do not include patient information, access credentials or confidential organisational records.

The repository does not yet assert an open reuse licence for community content. The governing group should agree the licence and contributor permissions before public release. See `LICENSE.md`. Third-party material retains its own terms.
