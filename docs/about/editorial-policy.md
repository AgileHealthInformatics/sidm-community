## Resource status

- **Working draft:** suitable for public discussion, with unresolved findings clearly labelled.
- **Reviewed:** checked by a named human reviewer against its stated scope and evidence. Review is not organisational endorsement.
- **Endorsed:** formally approved by the relevant body, with an explicit approval reference and date.
- **Archived:** retained for traceability and clearly marked as no longer maintained.

Every resource needs a stable identifier, owner, update date, review date and source versions. Until owners are appointed, the starter content remains a working draft. Do not label AI output as reviewed merely because it is plausible.

## Proportionate review

A correction of spelling can receive a lightweight editorial check. A change to an interpretation, recommendation or finding needs a competent reviewer other than its author. Changes claiming SIDM or BCS endorsement require the applicable organisational approval before publication.

Record agreements, dissent and uncertainty. An editor must not convert an AI-generated synthesis into a claim of community consensus.

## Sources and permissions

Link to authoritative specifications. Record version and clause references. Only publish source extracts, images, recordings or other material where permission or licensing allows it. Keep confidential records and personal or patient data outside the public repository and its history.

## Corrections

Raise a correction through the contribution route. The owner should assess its significance, update the page and record the reason in the pull request. Material errors should be identified visibly until corrected. Retain stable URLs where possible.
