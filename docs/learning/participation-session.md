Introduce the practical question and the relevant sources before demonstrating the analysis method. Give participants a small number of candidate findings to review, including opportunities to reject the AI interpretation.

Capture the source reference, assessment and rationale for each contribution. Invite participants to review the synthesis after the session and indicate whether they wish to be credited. Offer a facilitated route for people who do not use GitHub.

This page describes a proposed session format. It is not an event announcement or a record of a session already held.
