- Is the purpose and intended audience clear?
- Is the scope bounded and the publication status accurate?
- Are source versions, clauses and permissions recorded?
- Can each material claim be checked against the cited evidence?
- Are confirmed findings, proposals and uncertainties distinguished?
- Has a competent person checked the substantive interpretation?
- Is AI assistance disclosed and its output verified?
- Are disagreements retained and contributor attribution agreed?
- Is an owner assigned and a next review date set?
- Do links, tables and mobile layouts work?

Use this checklist proportionately. It supports editorial review and is not a certification or conformance assessment.
