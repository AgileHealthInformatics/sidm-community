This demonstrator illustrates how SIDM could develop a shared body of work on standards, interoperability and data management. It is a proposal for the group to consider, not an announcement of an agreed programme.

The pages show possible ways to organise learning resources, collaborative enquiries and AI-assisted review. The proposed responsibilities, participation arrangements and pilot would need to be agreed before any service or programme was launched.

## Pilot for consideration

[AI-assisted comparison of data definitions]({{ '/enquiries/data-definitions/' | relative_url }}) illustrates a possible first enquiry. Its scope, source material, participants and ownership have not been agreed. No completed analysis or review findings are claimed.

## Decisions before a launch

SIDM would need to agree the purpose and scope, ownership and hosting, editorial responsibilities, participation routes, permissions and publication arrangements. Any BCS endorsement would require the applicable approval.

The material is publicly accessible to demonstrate the proposal. Public accessibility does not make it an official SIDM website or an active community service.
