SIDM can bring practitioners and standards specialists together to examine real problems, review evidence and create resources others can use. This repository is a starting point for that approach. Participation can range from checking one claim to helping lead an enquiry.

## First proposed enquiry

[AI-assisted comparison of data definitions]({{ '/enquiries/data-definitions/' | relative_url }}) is a bounded pilot proposal. The community would choose the source material and verify each candidate finding. No review findings or organisational commitments are claimed in this starter edition.

## What this site contains

A participation model, editorial guidance, reusable templates, a pilot enquiry and an AI review method. The learning archive and standards guide are deliberately small; they should grow through reviewed contributions.
